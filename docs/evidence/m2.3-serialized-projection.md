## Behavior established

and the marked store, and assembles one exact generation. The workflow contract
places this preparation after the project lock; these local tests do not prove a
live workflow acquired that lock. Missing or malformed policy, ownership, store,
run or prepared-state data refuses before mutation. Restoration validates the
captured identities and exact allowed file bytes; it restores derived URL builders
rather than caller-supplied executable functions.

Finish consumes that prepared generation and the official-step observation. It
requires actual served-byte readiness and fresh head/order/readiness guards before
every comment mutation, retry and fallback. A public error label or a replayed
HTTP422 does not authorize fallback. Cancellation starts no new mutation. A genuine
validated HTTP201 acceptance survives disposal expiry and finite cleanup warnings;
an earlier foreign reaction cannot turn a deferred result into created or conceal
an accepted comment, because the producer freezes its genuine result before promise
fulfillment. Unknown sent outcomes remain failures with repair guidance.

and failed32 evidence are retained. ADR0031 and the projection contract record the
approved distinction between a sent uncertain operation and an unsent deferral.
